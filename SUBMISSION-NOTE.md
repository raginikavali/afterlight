# Decision-maker

A city innovation-office lead or urban-planning budget holder deciding
whether to fund a district-level pilot — someone who has seen "smart
city" pitches overpromise before and will ask, immediately, who is
accountable if an algorithm starts making calls about public space.

# The one choice made to win them

Built "Operator Review" as a working feature, not a slide — every match
the system proposes stops at a human approve/hold/reject before anything
happens, so the pitch answers the decision-maker's hardest objection by
demonstration inside the product itself, rather than asking them to take
accountability on faith.
